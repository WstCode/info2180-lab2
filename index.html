My New World!

September 30, 2026 by Lael Brown

This week I finally started a brand new survival world, and the very first challenge was deciding where to build my base. After wandering for a few in-game days, I settled on a small hill beside a river, which gave me easy access to water, trees, and a great view of the sunrise. The area also had plenty of animals nearby, so finding food was much easier than I expected.

I built the walls out of oak logs and stone bricks, then added a sloped roof so the house would look warm and inviting. I also put a few windows along the front so I could watch the sun come up each morning. Inside, I squeezed in a bed, a furnace, a crafting table, and plenty of chests. It is not a huge house, but it already feels like a proper home and gives me a safe place to return to after exploring.

The reality of Exploring

October 5, 2026 by Lael Brown

After a few days of exploring the surrounding area, I found a small cave system that looked promising. I decided to venture inside, hoping to find some valuable resources. However, I quickly realized that the cave was much more dangerous than I anticipated. The narrow passages and sudden drops made it easy to get lost, and I had to be careful not to fall into any hidden pits.

Despite the challenges, I managed to find some iron ore and a few coal deposits. I also encountered a few hostile mobs, which made me realize that I need to be better prepared for future explorations. Overall, it was an exciting adventure, but I learned that I need to be more cautious and plan my trips more carefully.
